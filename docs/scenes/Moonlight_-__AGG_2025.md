# Moonlight

## Description

_As the clouds disperse, our character finds shelter in a shepherd’s hut. Through the missing roof, it is possible to gaze at the beautiful waxing moon, while the sun slowly rises in the east. In the distance, our character sees the crossroads where they had planned to meet their beloved._
