# Ancient grave

## Description

_Wandering through the graveyard, a lone sepulchre draws the attention of our character. A decayed statue depicting a knight in armour has suffered centuries of erosion; a shadow of its former glory. The epitaph on the tomb tells the tale of a nobleman of old, tragically departing from our world on the day of his marriage._
