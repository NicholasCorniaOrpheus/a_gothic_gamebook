## Description

_The charming figure of Ymir leads Sasha to a ruined castle in the woods. As the gates of the castle open, a beautiful, brightly-lit garden is revealed. Fair ladies and courtiers of a forgotten time entice Sasha to join them. Sasha forgets all of their worries and the memory of their beloved Rune is merely a distant echo._
