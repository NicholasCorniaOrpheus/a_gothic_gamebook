# Recognition of the evil sorceress

## Description

_After being hit by the pebble, the strange bird emits a sharp sound like a woman’s scream. Is it possible? Instead of a bird, Sasha is confronted by a furious witch with pale skin and scarlet curls. Shrieking curses in an unknown language, the figure approaches. Sasha runs away as fast as possible._
