# Elf of the woods creates charming illusion

## Description

_Walking through the thick undergrowth, Sasha hears the sound of a small brook. While slaking their thirst, Sasha notices the ethereal sounds of an ancient song. The indistinguishable words of the song lead Sasha to a state of enchantment. While losing consciousness, a fairylike creature emerges from the water._
