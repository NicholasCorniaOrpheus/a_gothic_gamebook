# Wandering through the ruins

## Description

_Rune arrives at an abandoned summer estate, a desolate castle once the splendor of a noble family. Rain has been falling incessantly, having helped Rune’s escape from their greedy tutor. While wandering through the ruins, Rune seeks a refuge for the night._
