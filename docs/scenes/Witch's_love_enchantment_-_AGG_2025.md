# Witch's love enchantment

## Description

_Ymir, exhausted by the futility of trying to persuade Sasha to forget their lover, becomes enraged. Ymir’s charming facade disintegrates, and they utter primordial and mysterious syllables - an ancient language lost to the memory of man. An ancestral enchantment is binding Sasha’s soul to the power of Ymir._
