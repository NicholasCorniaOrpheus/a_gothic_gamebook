# Witches' ritual

## Description

_The strange and mesmerising litany leads Sasha to an ancient well surrounded by trees of great age. A group of hooded figures surround the mossy stones, drawing water from the well with a frayed rope. One figure turns towards Sasha, revealing pale skin, crimson hair, and a supernatural beauty. Ymir offers them a cup of the mystical liquid._
