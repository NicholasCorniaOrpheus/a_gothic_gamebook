# Abandoned horse in the forest

## Description

_Arriving at a clearing, the feeble light of dawn reveals a noble steed. Although richly bridled, there is no trace of a rider. Perhaps the lonely rider has lost his way, or was captured by the king of elves? Carefully approaching the horse, Sasha is unnerved by the distant sound of singing female voices._
