# Prayer of the Hermit

## Description

_After having listened to Rune’s story, the hermit recites a prayer in a low and solemn tone. The words of the invocation reveal a glimpse of Vanadis’s past, echoing the same miseries of our character’s love story. Rune regains strength and faith. After some kind words of farewell, they resume their journey._
