# Wandering through the forest

## Description

_At the boundaries of the ancient forest, a lonely traveller advances on a desolate road. Sasha has left the safety of their native village in order to rescue their beloved Rune from an unwilling marriage. Staring at the impenetrable darkness of the woods, they realise how hard it will be to reach the other side by dawn…_
